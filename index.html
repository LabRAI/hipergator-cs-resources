Before applying

Have these details ready

If Dr. Yu or fsu-compsci-dept is not available in the form, contact the department administrator or UFRC Support instead of selecting another sponsor or group.

SSH public key

Create the key before opening the form

Linux or macOS

ssh-keygen -t ed25519 -C "your_institutional_email" -f ~/.ssh/id_ed25519_hipergator
cat ~/.ssh/id_ed25519_hipergator.pub

Windows PowerShell

ssh-keygen -t ed25519 -C "your_institutional_email" -f $env:USERPROFILE/.ssh/id_ed25519_hipergator
type $env:USERPROFILE/.ssh/id_ed25519_hipergator.pub

Upload the file ending in .pub. Never upload or send the private key. The public key may be required even when you plan to start with a browser-based interface.

Submit the request

Identify your faculty collaboration in Comments

  1. Open Request HiPerGator Account.
  2. Use Federated Account Request if your institution participates in federation. Otherwise, ask UFRC Support which account path applies.
  3. Enter your legal name, institutional email, and actual organization. Select Dr. Weikuan Yu in the Group Sponsor field for fsu-compsci-dept.
  4. In Comments, state the full name of the professor you work with. Do not write only "Computer Science Department."
  5. Paste the requested information or upload the public key as directed by the form.
  6. Open the confirmation email, accept the terms and conditions, and complete any remaining verification step.
  7. Wait for sponsor approval. Account creation usually takes two to three business days after approval.

Comments template

I am a [FSU student/staff member OR student/researcher at HOME INSTITUTION] working with Professor [FULL NAME]. I am requesting HiPerGator access to fsu-compsci-dept for [PROJECT, COURSE, OR PURPOSE]. My institutional email is [EMAIL].

Replace every bracketed field. External applicants should use their home institution and institutional email.

Department membership

Add an existing account to fsu-compsci-dept

  1. Confirm that your HiPerGator account is active and record your exact username.
  2. Open a request at UFRC Support asking to add your username to fsu-compsci-dept.
  3. Include your full name, institutional email, home institution if applicable, the professor you work with, and the purpose of the request.
  4. Wait for department approval through UFRC Support.
  5. After confirmation, verify the group and Slurm account on HiPerGator before submitting production work.

FAQ

Common questions

Which professor should I name?

Name the professor whose project, course, or research work you are joining. This may be different from the Group Sponsor. If you work with more than one professor, list the primary contact first.

Do I need a new account to use the department allocation?

No. If your account already exists, request group membership through UFRC Support and provide your existing HiPerGator username.

What if the department group is not visible?

Contact the department administrator or UFRC Support and state that you are requesting access to fsu-compsci-dept. Do not select another sponsor or group.

Can an external student use this guide?

Yes. Use a federated home-institution account when available. If federation is unavailable, ask UFRC Support which account path applies. The FSU professor you work with should confirm the work before you apply.
